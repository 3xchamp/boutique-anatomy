# Paul's Boutique Anatomy

An interactive anatomy of *Paul's Boutique* (Beastie Boys, 1989) — the samples under the album, mapped on one clock.

**Live page:** https://3xchamp.github.io/boutique-anatomy/

## What it is

- **125 sample connections** to **121 distinct sources**, **122 with verified start times**, drawn from WhoSampled's documented connections and checked one by one. The 3 connections without a verified start are listed under their tracks rather than guessed onto the chart.
- The album itself plays *conducted, not hosted*: the page chains **23 official YouTube uploads** (Beastie Boys - Topic, including the 9 separate parts of "B-Boy Bouillabaisse") under a single album clock — 53:07 as chained here. Nothing is re-hosted.
- A decade-braid chart of where the samples live (1970s sources supply most of the sampled seconds), hover-to-identify, click-to-seek, decade filters, a "Right Now" view of what's sounding at the playhead, artist search with jump-to-moment, and per-sample doors out to the original songs.

## Honesty notes

- Sample **start times are documented facts**; sample **lengths are estimates** (a strand runs until the next documented sample enters, capped at 30 seconds, or to the track end where a source is marked "(and throughout)").
- "Hey Ladies" shows 17 verifiable connections; community counts run higher (~21). The difference is flagged on the page, not padded.
- One placement is disputed (Malcolm McLaren's "Buffalo Gals"): WhoSampled puts it in "A.W.O.L."; the Beastie Boys' own sample list puts it in "59 Chrystie Street." The page follows WhoSampled and flags it.
- One source door is a re-recording (Bernard Herrmann's "The Murder" is the 1997 Varèse Sarabande performance, not the 1960 film recording) — flagged on the page.

Companion piece to [All Day Anatomy](https://3xchamp.github.io/all-day-timeline/), the same treatment for Girl Talk's *All Day*.
